# Choose the source capability the inquiry needs

Use when a commission needs several acquisition paths, a provider comparison, or material beyond ordinary text search. Start with tools and material already available under the task's authority. A product name is not a capability contract.

| Need | Useful operation | What still needs checking |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Current outside facts or unfamiliar mechanisms | Open-web discovery, followed by primary-source reading | Date, version, coverage, and whether a snippet includes the deciding conditions |
| A mechanism remembered without its project name | Search a selected corpus by meaning and exact phrases; inspect the original item | Corpus membership, stale or duplicate derivatives, scope filters, and source versus generated synthesis |
| Exact conditions in a known source | Read or extract the original page, document, source file, or retained snapshot | Extraction omissions, tables/footnotes, stable locators, and what was actually read |
| A sustained comparison or investigation | An authorized research executor with its supported continuation and result retrieval | Readable sources, competing explanations, gaps, and coordinator repair work; completion alone is not acceptance |
| A visual, audible, timed, or interactive relationship | Inspect the actual medium with available viewing, playback, or interaction tools | Behavior over time, controls, editable state, and representative inputs; a textual description may not expose the effect |

These operations can be composed; they are not required service slots or a registry to install. Provider-specific behavior should remain visible when it changes evidence or experience. Prefer the existing supported path before adding an adapter. New capability can be justified by a worthwhile commission that becomes possible or substantially deeper; the current tools need not fail completely first.

For corpus retrieval, distinguish the live source, captured evidence, and search projection. They may share infrastructure while having different update and retention semantics. Index metadata supports selection; it need not be the full record of authorship, revision, adoption, or validity. Keep those distinctions in a rereadable source when the conclusion needs them. A relevance score is not factual confidence, and scores from different providers are not automatically comparable.

Evaluate retrieval at the actual question: unknown project names, exact technical strings, paraphrases, missing qualifiers, outdated duplicates, and no supported answer can expose different limitations. Do not claim semantic benefit from a literal-search toy or from a successful API call. If remote upload, credentials, paid execution, or retention are unavailable or unauthorized, finish the supported local comparison and identify exactly which inference still needs a live observation.

For creative work, describe the relationship the reference contributes, then carry it into the candidate's actual medium. A still frame cannot establish a motion sequence, and a playback cannot establish editability. Reuse the active production method for making and checking the work.
