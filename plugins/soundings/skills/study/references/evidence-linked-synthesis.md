# Keep a synthesis usable through revision

Use this when a substantial comparison or explanation will be reused, challenged,
or carried into consequential work. Ordinary synthesis can stay in prose. Keep
the current project's record and authority; a research artifact is a view of the
judgment, not a new master plan or permission to act.

## Make the deciding relationship inspectable

Work around the live question. For each conclusion that changes the answer,
make it possible to recover:

- the observation, exact passage, or artifact on which it rests;
- the conditions under which that evidence applies;
- the inference that connects it to this question;
- relevant counterevidence and a competing explanation;
- which later judgments or proposed actions depend on it.

These distinctions can be a few connected sentences. Do not atomize a useful
explanation into a registry of facts. A synthesis should still explain something:
why the sources differ, what relationship accounts for their observations, or
what this project should now understand or do. Exact citations make a claim
checkable; they do not make its interpretation correct.

Keep observation, inference, proposal, and judgment distinct. A source's
recommendation can be evidence about its own design without becoming a local
decision. Two snapshots, authors, or URLs do not establish two independent
observations. Preserve version, scope, and observation lineage in the relevant
conditions rather than treating citation count as corroboration.

## Keep counterevidence attached to the answer

Compare the sources on the same consequential distinction. Read the qualification
that could reverse a conclusion, including distant notes where needed. A passage
that challenges an inference belongs next to that inference even when the
preferred route still wins. If the evidence cannot distinguish two explanations,
keep the uncertainty and identify the observation that would separate them.

For example, one document may say a store commits successfully while a
separate-process probe loses the value. A second release may persist committed
values. The useful explanation is conditional on release and process boundary;
"the store is unreliable" merges different claims. A recommendation to own
persistence depends on those runtime conditions and the application's recovery
contract. It need not govern another tool using the durable release.

This example is constructed. It illustrates the inference and its dependency,
not a result about a real provider.

## Revise the affected judgment and return to the work

When new evidence changes a premise, retain the old observation at its historical
scope, mark the superseded interpretation clearly, and revisit its descendants.
Supporting and challenging evidence can both change a judgment. Check the real
plans, code, content, and acceptance criteria as well as any recorded links:
undeclared dependencies will not appear in a mechanical query.

Preserve independent goals and findings. A changed runtime fact can reopen a
persistence recommendation while leaving the user's offline requirement intact.
Decide from the new evidence whether to revise, retain with different grounds,
or retire each affected conclusion. Then carry the authorized change into the
actual deliverable and revisit the initiating use scene, following
[returning findings to the work](../../search/references/returning-to-the-work.md).
A dependency warning or corrected report alone does not complete that work.

## Optional local workpaper

When repeated citation assembly and dependency checking deserve deterministic
support, [the workpaper CLI](../../search/references/workpaper-format.md) connects
an explicitly authored JSON record to existing evidence snapshots. It can inspect
exact cited ranges, render a standalone Markdown workpaper with source excerpts
and counterevidence, and report the declared descendants of a changed finding or
source reference. Search, Study, Explore, and Shape can use the same bounded
artifact without becoming a required sequence.

The author supplies the reasoning, conditions, relationships, and status. The
tool supplies exact source text and mechanical checks. Its inventory is **cited
coverage**, not evidence of what an agent read; a successful check does not prove
truth, sufficient context, source independence, source freshness, or adoption.
No snapshot or project artifact is changed by an inspection or impact query.

Use the existing project record directly when that is sufficient. If a workpaper
is useful, keep it and its sources in the task's authorized local location;
source locators, quotations, and authored judgments may be private. A self-contained
render is a selected disclosure of those contents. Review it before any separately
authorized transfer or publication. The tool does not retain material automatically,
fetch sources, upload, delegate, or enforce the user's permissions.
