# Shared working context

Search, Study, Explore, Shape, and any active domain method continue one task. Each Soundings capability can own a complete inquiry or contribute inside a larger commission. They are independent entrypoints, not required stages or separate versions of the user's intent.

Keep a compact working understanding, usually in context rather than as a new file:

- **Outcome:** what the work is trying to make true and the qualities that matter.
- **Settled choices:** decisions the user or authoritative project contract actually made, and their relevant conditions.
- **Live proposals:** mechanisms and interpretations introduced by the agent or still being tested.
- **Evidence and unknowns:** facts, observations, references, and unresolved conditions that could change the next step.
- **Inquiry question:** the open choice or relationship this work is trying to resolve, when one exists.
- **Authority and next action:** what may be done now and what genuinely remains the user's decision.

Do not confuse these categories while compressing or handing off work. Local implementation is not acceptance; an accepted baseline can coexist with still-open dimensions; a direct observation is not a universal mechanism claim; a recommendation is not a settled choice; a successful probe is not adoption; a user preference about one artifact is not whole-project approval; a committed implementation is not deployment.

Keep local acceptance distinct from what the project has implemented. Preserve which references were actually observed, which were only discovered, and the conditions behind an accepted part or an observed result. Do not turn one rejection into a permanent prohibition or one external implementation into a local requirement. Read [proactive inquiry and reference choice](../skills/search/references/proactive-inquiry.md) when this distinction affects an open substantial choice.

When the commission asks what in an existing project is worth pursuing, begin from the accepted outcome, current artifacts and use, settled choices, and live proposals. Discovering the question is part of the work rather than a reason to return a backlog. Choose by consequence and pursue the strongest question to a usable result within the current authority. Do not manufacture an unrelated demo because it is easier to show than the project's real uncertainty.

At a substantial still-open product, form, interaction, visual, architecture, or implementation choice, relevant outside discovery can be part of the work even when the user did not request it. A clear goal or confident local proposal is not evidence that the solution space is settled. Reuse still-applicable references, reopen discovery when the current choice changes, and proceed directly for clear small repairs or faithful implementation of genuinely settled choices. Discovery does not require a named gap; it is not automatic for every substantial task.

Switching Skills must not reset prior choices, repeat questions already answered, or require a new brief. Pass only the context that changes the receiving method's decisions. A worker or external model does not inherit the task automatically; provide the minimum sufficient, privacy-safe contract when delegation is authorized.

Keep action completion separate from commission completion. A query can be finished while synthesis remains; a choice can be settled while its authorized implementation remains. Carry forward the full requested outcome, important findings not adopted, competing explanations, and the reason a route was deferred. Do not equate “a next step exists” with delivery, or invent additional work after the commission is satisfied.

Evidence reuse needs its own context: source identity and date, what was read, whether it was observed directly, source conditions, the interpretation made, and the project scope in which it mattered. Store source material and your own interpretation distinctly. A past rejection is conditional evidence about the observed output, not a permanent user preference or a prohibition on every related idea. When the question changes, reopen affected interpretations and leave room for new material.

Persist this understanding only when the project already needs cross-session continuity or a durable decision record. Update the established authority instead of creating a new ledger. Keep private continuity outside public project documentation, and preserve the reason and conditions behind a choice so later evidence can reopen only what it actually affects.

For a continuing research question, the existing record can preserve why it was worth asking, the conditional judgment and evidence it depends on, what effect was adopted or retained, the observed outcome, a promising unused clue, and what would reopen it. Include only conditions that changed or remain material to later work. A few lines are enough. Do not turn a provisional explanation into a permanent project identity or create a new ledger to satisfy this list.

When returned findings must change a project, use the [receiver continuation method](../skills/search/references/returning-to-the-work.md). When evidence must travel to another directory or harness, use the [research handoff method](../skills/search/references/research-handoffs.md) as needed. Its optional packet is an explicit selected copy of evidence and a brief, not a replacement authority, worker runtime, or automatic memory.
