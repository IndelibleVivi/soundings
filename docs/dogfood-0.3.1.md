## Correction and source behavior

Version `0.3.1` corrects the product claim made around `0.3.0` rather than erasing its useful work or historical evidence:

- Study now discovers a consequential unresolved question from an existing project's accepted outcome and artifacts, then pursues it to a result the project can use. A repository summary, backlog, plan, or detached demonstration is explicitly incomplete.
- Shared working context carries that commission across Search, Study, Explore, Shape, and active domain methods without adding a router or mandatory pipeline.
- Shape treats a candidate described by the owner as unintelligible, unrelated, or not meaningful product progress as a failure of representative role. It removes that role and its active descendants, preserves independent maintenance at honest scope, and returns to the accepted outcome for the replacement result.
- Explore still inspects the medium on which an accepted creative effect depends, but it no longer promotes motion, controls, or randomness merely to make a probe appear substantial.

- `evidence.py`, `packet.py`, their deterministic contracts, and the portable-handoff reference remain implemented as optional maintenance. They are not a headline capability or evidence of product growth.
