Jiandi Kai is a renamed, content-subset build of LXGW WenKai 1.522, Regular and Medium. Copyright and SIL OFL 1.1 retained in OFL-WenKai.txt. Release and original download URLs in KAI-SOURCES.json. Used for Chinese headings and short garden inscriptions.
